Privacy Policy

What information this website collects, how it's used, and the third-party services it relies on.

Effective Date: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected and used through brandonreeveslcsw.com, the informational website for my private psychotherapy practice.

This policy applies only to this website. It does not apply to the confidential clinical records created if you become a client. Those records are governed separately by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and the Notice of Privacy Practices provided at the beginning of treatment.

Not for Emergencies. This website, email, and the contact form are not monitored in real time. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency or are in immediate danger, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.

Information You Provide

If you contact me by email, telephone, or the website contact form, I receive whatever information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Information you include in your message

I use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, answer questions, or schedule a consultation. I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Please do not include detailed medical or mental health information in the contact form or by email. These methods are intended only for scheduling and general questions and should not be considered secure methods for clinical communication.

Information Collected Automatically

Like virtually all websites, this site automatically generates limited technical information needed to operate securely.

My hosting providers and website infrastructure (including Netlify and Cloudflare) may automatically collect technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages requested
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Basic diagnostic and security information

This information is used for purposes such as:

  • operating the website
  • improving reliability and performance
  • preventing abuse or malicious activity
  • troubleshooting technical issues

I do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral advertising tools, or similar visitor tracking services.

Third-Party Services

Certain portions of this website rely on services provided by third parties. When those services load, your browser may communicate directly with those providers according to their own privacy practices.

Google Fonts

Google Fonts is used to display the site's typography. Loading pages that use Google Fonts may result in your browser communicating with Google, including transmission of your IP address.

Google Maps

The Contact page includes an embedded Google Map to display my office location. Google may collect technical information necessary to provide this service.

Google Forms

The website contact form is hosted through Google Forms.

Information submitted through the contact form is stored within a Google Workspace account that I control. Google processes this information in accordance with its own privacy practices.

My Google Workspace account is covered by a current HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Google. However, because website contact forms and email are not intended for transmitting confidential clinical information, visitors should avoid submitting detailed health information through these methods.

JaneApp

When you select Book Free Consultation, you are redirected to JaneApp for scheduling.

Once you leave this website, your use of JaneApp is governed by JaneApp's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Cloudflare

This website uses Cloudflare to provide content delivery, security, and protection against malicious traffic. As part of providing these services, Cloudflare processes technical information such as IP addresses and other connection data.

Cookies

This website does not intentionally use advertising or behavioral tracking cookies.

However, third-party services used by this website — including Google services, Cloudflare, and JaneApp — may use cookies or similar technologies as part of providing their services. Those cookies are governed by the privacy policies of the respective providers.

Data Retention

Information submitted through the website is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain appropriate business records, or comply with applicable legal, ethical, or professional obligations.

Data Security

I take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information submitted through this website.

However, no method of transmitting information over the Internet or storing electronic data can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults and is not directed toward children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Your Choices

If you would like to know what contact information I have received through a website inquiry, or wish to request that it be corrected or deleted, please contact me.

I will honor reasonable requests unless I am required to retain the information for legal, ethical, or legitimate business purposes.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically as the website evolves.

The Effective Date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
